## What is it?

It's an app that allows you to record your 3 moods throughout the day. Why 3 you may ask? Because based on my personal experience, even in my worst, mood-swing days, the maximum number of moods I could feel is 3. And that's 1 mood in the morning 🌞, afternoon 🌈, and night 🌜.

## How to run it?

For now it's Android-only, and everything lives on your own phone.
No account, no internet needed 🫧

1. Open the project in Android Studio
2. Plug in your phone (or start an emulator)
3. Press the green ▶ Run button, and that's it 👒

Want a ready-to-install file instead? The APK will be in the
Releases tab once the first version is out.

## How did it get here?

I decided to use iterative software development life cycle instead of waterfall because I don't want to feel overwhelmed with all the ideas and designs that I have to prepare beforehand. Every tiny step is what helps me to enjoy building this app without feeling any pressures. Always celebrate every accomplished iteration with an ice cream 🍦 and (don't forget) a **productive** mood stamp in the app <img src="app/src/main/res/drawable/ic_mood_productive.webp" width="20" alt="productive mood">.
